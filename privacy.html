Privacy Policy

KnockoutStyle LLC · Last updated: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how KnockoutStyle LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information in connection with our employee scheduling and SMS notification program.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information from employees who participate in our scheduling notification program: name, mobile phone number, and work schedule information. This information is provided directly by the employee or their employer during onboarding.

How We Use Information

Mobile phone numbers are used solely to send work schedule notifications, shift change alerts, and related workplace communications via SMS to employees who have consented to receive them.

SMS Messaging Disclosures

  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with, or sold to, any third parties.
  • Message frequency varies based on scheduling activity (typically 1–10 messages per week).
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of employment.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties, except with service providers strictly as necessary to deliver messages (e.g., our SMS delivery provider), or as required by law.

Data Retention and Security

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above and protect it using reasonable administrative and technical safeguards.
